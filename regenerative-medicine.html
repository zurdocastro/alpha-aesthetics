What We Mean By Regenerative

Treating the Cause, Not the Symptom

Regenerative medicine works with the systems your body already uses to repair and regulate itself — its hormones, its signalling peptides, and the nutrients those processes depend on — rather than masking a symptom and moving on.

At our Alpharetta practice that means three things: bioidentical hormone optimization, compounded peptide therapy, and intravenous nutrient therapy. Every one of them begins with an evaluation by a licensed provider, and lab work wherever your levels should be measured rather than guessed at. Nothing is prescribed or dispensed before that.

About outcomes. Regenerative protocols are individual. What is appropriate for you — and whether anything here is appropriate at all — is determined at your consultation, not in advance. We do not promise a specific result, and individual results vary.

What We Offer

Bioidentical Hormone Optimization

Fatigue, weight that will not move, brain fog, poor sleep, low libido and mood changes get written off as stress or age. Often they are hormonal, and most people are never tested.

We use EvexiPEL bioidentical pellets, placed under the skin at the upper hip in a short in-office procedure. They release steadily for three to six months — no daily pill, no patch, and none of the peaks and troughs those produce. The dose is calculated from your own pre-pellet labs, and a follow-up panel afterwards confirms it landed where it was aimed rather than assuming it did.

Sermorelin is also available. It prompts your own pituitary gland to release more growth hormone, which is the important distinction — it is not synthetic HGH, it works with your body's own feedback loops.

Compounded Peptide Therapy

Peptides are short chains of amino acids — the signalling molecules your body uses to tell cells to repair, recover and regulate. Ours are compounded for the individual patient by a licensed compounding pharmacy, on a prescription written after an individual assessment.

We are not a reconstituted-product shop. A prescription is part of the process, not an obstacle to it, and we would rather you understood why than took our word for it. That is what the education session is for: how compounding actually works, what a compounding pharmacy does, and the questions worth asking any provider before you consider anything.

IV Nutrient Therapy

Vitamins, minerals and antioxidants delivered straight into the bloodstream, which is the whole point of giving them this way — nothing is lost crossing the gut. Administered by our clinical team in a comfortable, private setting in about forty-five minutes.

Add-ons are available to tailor an infusion, including glutathione, which is poorly absorbed by mouth and is one of the clearest cases for the intravenous route.

Common Questions

Regenerative Medicine FAQ

Is there a consultation before any treatment?

Yes, always. Every new patient receives a thorough consultation to assess your goals and create a customized treatment plan. Nothing is prescribed or dispensed before a licensed provider has reviewed your history.

How do I know whether I am a candidate?

That is decided at your consultation, from your health history and — where it applies — your lab work, not from a questionnaire. Hormone pellets, for example, are dosed from your own pre-pellet panel, so the labs come before the decision. If a protocol is not appropriate for you, your provider will say so.

What should I expect before and after?

It depends on the protocol. Hormone pellets are a short in-office procedure under local anaesthetic, with follow-up labs afterwards to confirm the dose. Peptides are prescribed and shipped to you rather than administered here. An IV infusion takes about forty-five minutes with no downtime. Your provider walks you through the specifics for your plan at the consultation.

Which regenerative services do you currently offer?

Bioidentical hormone optimization (EvexiPEL pellets, sermorelin and testosterone injections), compounded peptide therapy on prescription, and IV nutrient therapy. We also offer an education-only consultation on MUSE cell science, which is a conversation about the research rather than a treatment.

Do you treat men as well as women?

Yes. Our hormone, peptide and wellness protocols are built for both, with separate lab panels and separate dosing for each.

Do you accept insurance?

We do not bill insurance. However, we provide receipts you can submit for potential reimbursement if your plan allows. We also accept CareCredit financing, subject to a credit check.
