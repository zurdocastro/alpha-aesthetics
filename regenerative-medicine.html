Before You Decide

Start With an Evaluation, Not an Assumption

Most people do not come to us first. They come after the rest, the anti-inflammatories, the physical therapy, maybe the injections — and after being told the choice is between surgery and learning to live with it.

Regenerative medicine is an evolving field. Some approaches have a growing body of research behind them and others are still being studied. What we offer is not a promise of a result. It is a careful, individual evaluation of whether any of it is appropriate for you — and a straight answer either way.

Common Questions

Questions Worth Asking

What is regenerative medicine?

It is a broad and evolving area of medicine concerned with supporting the body's own repair processes rather than only managing symptoms. It covers a range of different approaches, which differ in how much research supports them and in how they are regulated. Your clinician will explain exactly what is being considered in your case.

Is this FDA-approved?

It depends entirely on the specific approach. Some are FDA-approved for specific, limited uses. Others are not approved to treat any particular condition. Regulatory status is not the same thing as clinical effectiveness, and we will tell you plainly which category anything discussed in your case falls into, before you decide anything.

How do I know whether I am a candidate?

Through an individual clinical evaluation, and only that way. Not everyone is a candidate. Your history, your current health and what you have already tried all factor in, and your clinician will give you a direct answer rather than leaving it open.

What happens at the consultation?

A licensed clinician reviews your symptoms and history, discusses what you have already tried, walks you through any options that may apply along with their risks and alternatives, and tells you whether moving forward makes sense for you. You are not committing to anything by coming in.

What does it cost?

That depends on what, if anything, turns out to be appropriate for you, so it is discussed during your consultation rather than quoted in advance from a price list. You will have clear numbers before you decide on anything.

Do you accept insurance?

We do not bill insurance. However, we provide receipts you can submit for potential reimbursement if your plan allows. We also accept CareCredit financing, subject to a credit check.

Who will I be seeing?

A licensed clinician, working under physician-supervised protocols. You can meet our team before you book.
