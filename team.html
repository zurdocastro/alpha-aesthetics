Who You Will See

Care Has a Name and a Face

Every evaluation at our Alpharetta practice is performed by a licensed clinician, under the oversight of our medical director. You should know who both of them are before you walk in — not after.

KJ

Kashif A. Janjua, MD

Medical Director
Board-Certified Internal Medicine

Dr. Kashif Janjua is a board-certified internal medicine physician and serves as Medical Director, responsible for clinical oversight, care standards, and the treatment protocols every patient is evaluated under.

His experience spans hospital, outpatient and telehealth settings, and he has worked with clinical teams on building the kind of systems that keep care safe and consistent rather than improvised.

Dr. Janjua’s focus is the bigger picture: that any recommendation fits a patient’s overall health, and that the evidence, the limits and the alternatives are all on the table before a decision gets made.

RA

Rachel Applegate, FNP-BC

Board-Certified Family Nurse Practitioner
Hospital Medicine & Regenerative Medicine

Rachel Applegate is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with several years of clinical experience and a strong background in hospital medicine. She brings a compassionate, patient-centered approach to regenerative medicine, emphasizing personalized care, patient education, and helping individuals feel comfortable, informed, and confident throughout their healthcare journey.

Passionate about improving quality of life, Rachel is dedicated to understanding each patient’s unique needs and helping them explore treatment options that align with their health and wellness goals.

Outside of medicine, Rachel is a proud mother of three who enjoys spending quality time with her family, exploring the outdoors, traveling, and tending to her garden.

Evaluations are performed by qualified, licensed clinicians under physician-supervised protocols. Treatment decisions are made together with you, with medical director oversight. Not every patient is a candidate; that is determined through individual clinical evaluation only.
